## 🎬 Video Overview & Recording Instructions

Agar aapko screen recording video banani hai (OBS Studio, Loom, ya phone screen recorder se):
1. Browser me website kholein: `http://localhost:3000` (ya live Render link).
2. Chrome ko Full Screen (`F11`) karein taaki browser tabs aur bookmarks na dikhein.
3. Microphone check karein aur neeche diye gaye script ko natural confidence ke saath bolein.
4. Har step ke saath cursor ko exact button par move karein aur click karein.
